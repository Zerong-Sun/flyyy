This game's data sources

Airport coordinates and basic info — OurAirports
Source: OurAirports open dataset (https://ourairports.com/data/)
License: Public Domain (Unlicense)
Note: Provides global airports' IATA/ICAO codes, names, coordinates and types. This game uses part of its ~85,000 airport records for the globe display and route analysis.

Route reference data — OpenFlights
Source: OpenFlights open routes and airlines dataset (https://openflights.org/data.html)
License: ODbL 1.0 (Open Database License); derivative databases built on it must be shared under the same license with attribution preserved.

ODbL key obligations summary:
1. Attribution: state that the data originates from OpenFlights.
2. Share-alike: derivative works built with this data must be published under ODbL or a compatible license.
3. No additional restrictions: you may not use technical measures to restrict others' use of the derivative database.
Full ODbL legal text: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1.0/

Flight schedules — synthetic reconstruction
Flight numbers and departure/arrival times in this game are algorithmically generated from public route data and aviation industry conventions, and are not a complete copy of any real airline timetable. All flight information is simulated to provide a plausible route experience.

Product price references — public retail estimates
Base prices are model estimates, not live market prices. References include mid-year average prices on public e-commerce platforms, category median unit prices from international trade yearbooks, and cross-city lifestyle survey data. Prices are frozen as of March 1, 2025.

Currency snapshot
USD is used as the internal settlement currency. Displayed local currency is converted to CNY at a snapshot rate, frozen on March 1, 2025. The rate is taken from that day's public mid-rate, for in-game reference only, and does not reflect live financial markets.

City background text
City overviews, history, geography, economy, food and travel notes are originally written by the team based on Wikidata structured facts (CC0). No Wikipedia or other CC BY-SA text sources are copied directly.

Important notice
The flight network, fares and schedules in this game are simulated or model estimates, and do not represent any airline's real operations or live booking advice. All product prices are fixed snapshots and do not reflect live market prices. This game is for entertainment purposes only.
The flight network is reconstructed from public aviation data and does not represent real ticket information.
